## What I Learned

This course introduced me to the fundamentals of cybersecurity and helped me understand how cyber threats can affect individuals and organizations.

Key topics I explored included:

- Cybersecurity threats and vulnerabilities
- Security breaches
- Phishing and social engineering
- Identity theft
- Personal data protection
- Password security and password managers
- Cookies and online privacy
- Cyber warfare
- Security awareness and safe online practices

## Practical Learning

I completed hands-on lab activities and worked through security breach scenarios. These activities helped me understand how security weaknesses can expose information and what organizations can do to prevent breaches.

## Biggest Takeaway

One of my biggest takeaways was realizing that cybersecurity is not only about protecting computers and networks. **People, accounts, devices, and personal information are all part of the security picture.**
