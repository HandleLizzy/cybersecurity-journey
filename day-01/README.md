Today I completed **Module 1 of the Cisco Networking Academy Introduction to Cybersecurity course**.

## What I Learned

I learned that **cybersecurity is the practice of protecting data, information, systems, networks, and digital resources from cyber threats and unauthorized access**.

One of the new terms I learned was **cybercriminals**. I also learned about different types of hackers, including **white-hat, black-hat, and grey-hat hackers**.

### Key Takeaways

* Cybersecurity is important for protecting an organization's information and systems.
* Cybercriminals can use technology to carry out harmful or illegal activities.
* Different types of hackers have different intentions and levels of authorization.
* Organizations need cybersecurity because their digital assets can be targeted by cyber threats.

### What I Found Challenging

I found it a little difficult to differentiate between **white-hat and grey-hat hackers**. This helped me understand how important authorization and intent are when distinguishing between different types of hackers.

### My Biggest Takeaway

What surprised me most was realizing that **every organization needs cybersecurity**. No matter the size or type of organization, its information and systems need to be protected.

This was my first step into my cybersecurity learning journey. I am starting with the fundamentals and plan to continue building my knowledge through courses, hands-on labs, and practical projects.
