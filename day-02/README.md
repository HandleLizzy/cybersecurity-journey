# Day 2 – Introduction to Cybersecurity

## What I Worked On

Continued my Cisco Networking Academy Introduction to Cybersecurity course.

## What I Learned

- Security breaches and their impact
- How attackers can gain unauthorized access
- Personal data exposure
- Identity theft
- Cookies and online tracking
- Cybersecurity vulnerabilities
- Ways organizations can prevent security breaches
- Cyber warfare

## Practical Work

Completed cybersecurity lab activities involving real-world security breach scenarios. I practiced identifying what information was exposed, understanding the weaknesses involved, and thinking about how the breaches could have been prevented.

## Key Takeaway

Cybersecurity is not only about protecting systems. Protecting **people, personal information, accounts, and devices** is equally important.

## Reflection

The course is changing the way I look at everyday technology and the amount of personal information that can be exposed online.
